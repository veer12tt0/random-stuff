Formula
of
Discipline

Introduction

Shorts, games, and many other activities nowadays are very fast-paced. This has made a huge impact on how we approach tasks and manage our time. Humans, who once used to hunt, explore, and stay curious, have now become nothing more than lazy creatures. The problem today isn't a lack of resources, but a lack of discipline. People today are inconsistent and unserious about their goals. They have lost the respect and credibility of their peers and elders, and constantly look for shortcuts.

The Formula

Discipline plays a crucial role in achieving success. Nowadays, one's discipline is measured by their consistency and patience. So, to arrive at an answer for how much discipline one has, I created a formula that takes all criteria into account and gives you a score of how much discipline you can expect to have.

{ [ ( Points_of_work 2 ) × Points_of_Exercise ] − Points_of_time_wasted }

{[(0 / 2) * 0] - 0} = 0

Here,

Points_of_work: The number of points assigned to your work. (1 hour of work = 1 point)

Points_of_Exercise: The number of points assigned to your exercise. (20 minutes of exercise = 1 point)

Points_of_time_wasted: The number of points assigned to the time wasted. (5 minutes of time wasted = 1 point)

Philosophy of the Formula

Discipline is not only about learning to repeat a task every day, but about maintaining a routine of tasks that benefits you and your health. The formula focuses on this by including your health alongside the work you do that you consider important or beneficial, ultimately leading to a better life. The formula is designed to be simple and easy to use, allowing anyone to assess their own discipline and make changes as necessary. Wasting time is never a good thing, and considering that, I have intentionally set the value of time wasted to 5 minutes per point. This is because wasting time is a habit that can be detrimental to one's discipline and overall well-being. By assigning a point value to wasted time, the formula encourages individuals to be more mindful of how they spend their time and to make better choices that will lead to improved discipline and productivity.

Conclusion

The discipline formula is a simple yet effective way to gauge your discipline. It takes into account the points of work, exercise, and wasted time to give a score that reflects the level of discipline of the person. This formula allows people to assess their own discipline and make whatever changes are necessary to improve it.
